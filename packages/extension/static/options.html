Start over

Erase every game from and from your graph, and start with an empty board.

This replaces the board repo's history with one fresh commit. It can't be undone. GitHub can take up to a day to remove the old squares from your graph; until then, new games go in another empty year.

Good to know

  • Signing in grants Commit Four access to your public repositories (GitHub has no narrower option for this); it only ever writes to the board repo you set up here. You can revoke it any time at github.com/settings/applications.
  • Boards are drawn in the newest past year (at least two years back) with no contributions on your graph, six boards per year, so your real activity keeps its look and can't change the board's colors. Start a game from your profile; Commit Four opens that year for you.
  • If your profile activity is private, only you can see the board.
  • Commit Four only writes to repos containing its .commit-four-board file, never force-pushes (except when you choose Start over), and keeps volumes tiny (about 130 commits per game).
  • Unofficial project; GitHub's abuse detection could still flag unusual activity. Use at your own risk.
